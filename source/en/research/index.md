## Directions I'd Like to Explore

> These are areas where I see overlap between what the School of Mining Engineering actually works on and my own technical background. **Just initial thoughts — the final direction will follow my advisor's projects.**

- **Data and monitoring in mine geohazard prevention** — the school works on mine disaster prevention, which depends heavily on monitoring data and early-warning judgment. If a group needs help organizing, analyzing, or building visualization/early-warning systems, that's right where I can contribute.
- **Smart mines / geological informatization** — turning survey data, geological maps, and mine-shaft data into digital, interactive tools is a clear trend. I've built web systems, so "turning data into usable tools" is familiar territory for me.
- **Data processing in mineral exploration** — exploration generates large volumes of data that need cleaning, management, and visualization. I don't do the geological interpretation, but I can handle the technical side of making the data usable.
- **Staying open** — the above are just where I see overlap. If my advisor's direction is something else entirely, I'm willing to learn and adapt.

2. **Produce at least one meaningful outcome** — a paper, an open-source tool, or a method actually used by my group
3. **Transition from "student" to "researcher"** — able to independently pose questions, design methods, and verify answers
